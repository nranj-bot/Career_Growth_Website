Meet the Founder of Roya by Nahal

Nahal Ranjbar

Engineer. Public speaker. Author.

The Story Behind Roya

At ten years old, Nahal and her family immigrated to the United States in pursuit of new opportunities. As a first-generation college student without generational wealth or an established professional network, she had to navigate college and the early stages of her career largely on her own.

While working multiple jobs to finance her education, Nahal sought out every opportunity she could. She became the first in her graduating class to secure an internship and went on to complete one every summer—each leading to a return offer. By graduation, she had multiple competing job offers.

Her journey showed her how powerful guidance and access can be—and how difficult it can be to succeed without them.

That's why she created Roya: to provide first-generation students and early-career professionals with the guidance, resources, and confidence to navigate their careers and build opportunities of their own.

Peace of Mind

I published my first book, Peace of Mind, at 17 — on finding peace through faith in God during the hardest, most uncertain moments. It came out of my own life long before Roya did, and it still shapes how I show up for the people I coach: steady, honest, and grounded, even when the path forward isn't clear yet.
